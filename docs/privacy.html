Bastion Road – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Bastion Road ("the game") is developed by Virabyan. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

We do not collect personal data

The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your campaign progress (completed missions and stars), your best Endless result, and your settings (language, sound on/off) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. App stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
