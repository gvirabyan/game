Bastion Road – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Bastion Road ("the game") is developed by Virabyan. This policy explains what data is collected when you play and why.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, which may also deliver ads from Unity Ads (mediation). To show and measure ads, these services may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

When required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy. On iOS you can also refuse tracking in the system prompt or in iOS Settings. Ads are never required to play: the only rewarded ad is optional.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example which missions are started and finished). It collects an app instance identifier, device and app information and these gameplay events. No names, emails or contacts are collected.

Third-party privacy policies

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase): policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Data stored on your device

Your campaign progress (missions and stars), best Endless result and settings (language, sound) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
